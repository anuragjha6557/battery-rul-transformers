Every method, over the whole life of a cell

Pick a cell and one forecast horizon h. Each method's curve is made of its forecasts issued h cycles earlier, drawn at the cycle they predict, so the whole life is covered by forecasts of the same length. All benchmarked methods are shown with their genuine held-out predictions: the model never trained on this cell. Toggle methods on and off to compare.

Predicted vs measured SOH

Each line = one method's h-step-ahead forecasts over the cell's life. Thick translucent yellow = measured SOH. ▲ = cycle after a long rest (regeneration), × = charger glitch (CALCE artifact). "+ planned rests" variants also know the rest schedule ahead (ADR-0016).

Forecast error over life

Absolute error |forecast − measured| at each target cycle, smoothed with an 11-cycle rolling mean so methods are comparable at a glance (hover shows the smoothed value).

Ranking at this horizon

RMSE and bias over every target cycle of this cell at the chosen horizon; interval coverage = share of measured values inside the method's calibrated 90% interval. RUL MAE is over every prediction point before end of life. The live model is trained on all three datasets at once; on NASA its RUL reacts strongly to each regeneration jump, while the per-dataset benchmark models are steadier there.

Remaining-life prediction over life

Predicted RUL at every point in the cell's life against the true remaining life (red dashed) and a ±20% cone. RUL does not depend on the forecast horizon.
