Predicted RUL (median and 90% interval) if the prediction had been made at each cycle, against the true remaining life where it is known. The dotted cone is ±20% of the truth. A good prognostic converges into the cone as the cell ages. On NASA, the spikes right after capacity jumps (recovery after long rests) show the model briefly reading the recovery as slower ageing; on CALCE the model is consistently pessimistic, as in the benchmark.

Forecast intervals are conformalized quantile regression (CQR) at 90%. Their coverage was checked on held-out cells; it holds only for cells that resemble the training data.

Upload a CSV with one row per charge/discharge cycle and these columns (extra columns are ignored):

  • cycle_idx: cycle number (integers, increasing; gaps are fine)
  • capacity_ah: measured discharge capacity of that cycle, in Ah

At least 10 cycles are required; 64 or more give the model its full input window. State of health is computed as capacity divided by the mean of the first 5 cycles, so start the file at the beginning of life.

Remaining useful life is the number of cycles until SOH falls to 80%.

Model. iTransformer-v2: single-channel iTransformer (inverted, variate-token attention) with direct multi-horizon quantile heads (h = 1, 10, 25, 50, 100 cycles) and a direct RUL quantile head. Intervals are calibrated with conformalized quantile regression on cells the model never trained on.

Training data. NASA PCoE (B0005–B0018), CALCE CS2 (LiCoO₂) and Toyota/MIT-Stanford (Severson 2019, 124 LFP cells), pooled; inputs are SOH history only, so any cycler's capacity log works.

Limitations. Intervals are distribution-free only when your cell resembles the training cells; the page warns when it does not (out-of-distribution check). RUL for chemistries, temperatures or protocols far from the training data is unreliable. Demo cells are cut at half of their life (at least 64 cycles); the Toyota demo is the held-out cell with the median error, so it is typical rather than best-case.

Evidence. Leakage-free benchmark of 18 model configurations on 3 datasets, design decisions and negative results: github.com/anuragjha6557/battery-rul-transformers.

Paper. Jha, Dorkar, Biswas, Emadi, “iTransformer Network Based Approach for Accurate Remaining Useful Life Prediction in Lithium-Ion Batteries,” IEEE ITEC 2024, doi:10.1109/ITEC60657.2024.10598898.
